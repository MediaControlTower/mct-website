MCT for Parents

Homework done. Chores finished. Time to feed a companion.

Media Control Tower brings your household’s movies, shows, music and photos together. Companion rewards are designed to celebrate what kids do away from the screen.

Reward homework, chores and cooking

Open Parent Rewards from your child’s Statistics screen. Choose Homework, Chores or Cooking, then award 5, 10, 15, 20 or 25 Dexterinoos. The coins go straight into that profile’s Treasure Chest, where kids can spend them on snacks for their companions.

On Firestick, press Down from Name or Feed to reach Parent Rewards and use the remote’s directional ring to enter your parent code. In the browser, enter your separate 6–8 digit parent PIN. Manage both under Settings → Connections & services → Parent rewards on the MCT computer.

No subscription

New companion sets will be free to download. Kids earn their coins and grow their companions over time. Coins and levels can’t be bought with real money.

We may offer optional paid skins in the future for families who want to change how MCT looks.

Private testing starts with families

Parents and caregivers will be the first people we invite to help shape how rewards work at home. Public downloads will come later.
